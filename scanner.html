What this is

Voidcraft Scanner turns a video of a real object into a block model made of the same small cubes as the game's ground. Your phone films and shows the result. The laptop does the heavy work and keeps the files.

How to use it

  1. Scan: say what it is, then film or upload a video.
  2. Model: spin it, ask for changes, compare versions.
  3. Import: saves a .cells file on the laptop for the game.
  4. History: reopen or delete earlier scans.

Filming tips

  • Slow circle round the object, 30–60 seconds.
  • Do a second loop higher up, looking down.
  • Even light, plain background, object filling most of the frame.
  • Solid things work best (rocks, stumps, sheds). Thin leaves and shiny surfaces don't.

Not done yet

  • The 3D shape is a placeholder: one frame, inflated into a slab.
  • Change requests understand a few keywords only.
  • The game can't load .cells files yet.
